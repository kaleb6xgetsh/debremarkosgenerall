HISTORY OF OUR SCHOOL

Debremarkos Secondary Preparatory School is one of the oldest and most respected educational institutions in Debre Markos and throughout GOJAM. It was founded in 1962 E.C. with the assistance of Indian educators, marking the beginning of a long tradition of quality learning and cultural exchange. From its earliest days, the school has played a key role in shaping the minds of young Ethiopians and inspiring a love of knowledge and progress.

The school's first Ethiopian director was Mr. Dilesa Zewude, who served faithfully for more than 20 years. His leadership and dedication helped establish strong educational values that continue to guide the school today. Over the decades, Debremarkos Secondary School has become a center of excellence, encouraging academic success, discipline, and creativity among its students.

Today, the school stands as a symbol of both HERITAGE and ADVANCEMENT. It continues to uphold its founding vision of providing inclusive, high-quality education that prepares students for higher learning and for meaningful contributions to their communities and the nation.
